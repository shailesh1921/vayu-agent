## 🔢 Position Sizing Formula
```
Risk per trade = 2% of capital (max)
Shares = (Capital × Risk%) ÷ (Entry - Stop Loss)
```
**Example:** ₹5L capital, 2% risk = ₹10,000 max loss. Entry ₹100, SL ₹95 → 2,000 shares.

## 📊 Volume Confirmation Rules
- **Breakout valid**: Volume > 2× 20-day average
- **Breakdown valid**: Volume > 1.5× average (selling pressure)
- **Pullback valid**: Volume < average (no supply)
- **Reversal valid**: Volume spike on reversal candle
- **Delivery % > 50%** on up-move = institutional buying
- **Delivery % < 30%** = speculative/intraday churn

## 🚫 Red Flags — Skip the Trade
- RSI > 80 on Daily (chasing extended move)
- Volume drying up on breakout (false break)
- FII aggressively selling the stock (bulk deals)
- Promoter pledge > 10% or declining holding
- Stock at 52-week high with no fundamental catalyst
- Penny stock / SME / <₹5 Cr daily turnover
- Earnings due in 3 days (unless earnings momentum play)
- VIX > 28 (market panic — intraday only)

## 🔄 Trade Management Rules
1. **Trail SL to breakeven** after Target 1 hit
2. **Book 50% at T1**, 30% at T2, trail 20% with 20 DMA/SuperTrend
3. **Time stop**: If no move in 5 sessions (swing) / 2 hours (intraday) → exit
4. **Structure stop**: Weekly close below 50 DMA / Daily close below 200 DMA → exit
5. **Never average down** — add only on confirmed breakout with volume
6. **Journal every trade** — entry, exit, rationale, emotion, lesson
